iPad setup

  1. Open an ordinary webpage in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add Bookmark → Save.
  3. Open Safari bookmarks and edit that bookmark.
  4. Name it Íslenska Solver.
  5. Delete its URL and paste the complete javascript: URL above.
  6. Save, return to a webpage, and tap the bookmark.

Tap again to hide; tap again to show. Pages with restrictive security policies may block bookmarklets: use another ordinary webpage. Cache and preferences are kept on this hosted origin.
